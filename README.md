# Masheeds

Masheeds is an Android music player for your local audio and the online Discover catalog.

## Install

Download the APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/playmhd/masheeds/releases) and open it on a device running Android 8.0 or later.

## Listen

- **Library** lets you play audio stored on your device offline. Tap **+** to add files, or go to **Settings → Library → Scan device** to find audio on your device.
- **Discover** lets you stream as a guest. Browse **Quick picks**, **Albums**, **Artists and reciters**, and curated **Collections**. Search by title, reciter, album, or alternate spelling. **Continue listening** and **Recently played** use listening history on your device.
- **Profile** shows your Google account and the tracks you’ve published in Discover. Sign in to sync **Liked tracks** and submit an MP3 for review. The submission form previews the MP3’s artwork and lets you choose a different thumbnail. To remove one of your tracks from Discover for everyone, open its menu under **Published tracks** and choose **Delete published track**. To sign out, go to **Settings → Account**.
- **Now playing** opens when you tap the mini player. Swipe up or tap **Up next** to see the full queue. Tap any track to play it. Hold and drag tracks above or below the current one to reorder them, or tap a handle for move options. Upcoming tracks can also be removed.

## Make it yours

- Save local and Discover tracks in a playlist on this device. Sign in to create a synced Discover playlist. Use the menu on a Discover track to add it to a playlist.
- When a Discover track offers **Download over Wi-Fi**, save it and open **Library → Downloads** to play or remove it.
- Switch between light and dark themes, choose English or Arabic, and turn navigation haptics on or off in **Settings**.
- Open **Settings → Library → Recently deleted** to see tracks removed from the app or a playlist in the last 30 days. Removing a track from Masheeds leaves its original audio file on your device.
